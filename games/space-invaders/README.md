# Space Invaders

![Space Invaders preview](assets/preview.svg)

Space Invaders is a free, single-file arcade shooter. Move your ship, clear increasingly dangerous alien waves, collect power-ups, and survive long enough to face the mothership.

## Play

- [Launch the game](index.html)
- [Watch the video preview](assets/preview.mp4)

Open `index.html` directly in a modern browser. The game has no build step, external dependencies, or installation requirements.

## Controls

| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Move left | `Left Arrow` or `A` |
| Move right | `Right Arrow` or `D` |
| Fire | `Space` or `Up Arrow` |
| Start or restart | `Enter` or `Space` |

Destroy enemies, avoid incoming fire, collect power-ups, and defeat the boss. Sound effects are generated by the browser and begin after your first key press.
